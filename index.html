🎯 How to Play

Forget reading big manuals. TicTacToe is all about instant fun:

  1. Player 1 chooses "X", Player 2 chooses "O".
  2. Take turns clicking on any empty square on the 3x3 grid.
  3. The first player to align three of their marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins!
  4. If all squares fill up with no winner, that's a draw – start a new round!

That's it. Simple, fast, and might just bring back some childhood memories.
